## Training Domain
**Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI & ML) using Python**

## Objective

The objective of today's training was to understand how to evaluate the results of K-Means Clustering and determine the optimal number of clusters using the Elbow Method. The session also focused on interpreting customer segments and understanding their practical applications in business.

---

## Tasks Assigned by the Industry Expert

The following tasks were assigned during today's training session:

1. Analyze the customer segments generated by K-Means Clustering.
2. Understand the role of cluster centroids.
3. Learn the importance of selecting an appropriate value of K.
4. Implement the Elbow Method for cluster evaluation.
5. Calculate and compare Inertia (WCSS) for different values of K.
6. Identify the optimal number of clusters from the Elbow Curve.
7. Interpret the business significance of different customer groups.

## Work Performed

During today's practical session, I completed the following activities:

- Analyzed the customer groups generated by the K-Means model.
- Studied the characteristics of different customer segments based on income and spending patterns.
- Observed the position of cluster centroids and understood how they represent each group.
- Ran the K-Means algorithm for different values of K.
- Calculated the Inertia (WCSS) for each clustering result.
- Plotted the Elbow Curve to compare different cluster values.
- Identified the optimal number of clusters using the elbow point.
- Compared the clustering results obtained with different values of K.
- Discussed how customer segmentation can support business decision-making.
